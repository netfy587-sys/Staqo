Stay safe online: five simple steps

Most online problems come from a few common mistakes. The fixes are simple, and you can do most of them in one afternoon.

1

🔑 Use strong, unique passwords

Reusing one password means a single leak can expose everything. Use a long passphrase for each account and let a reputable password manager remember them.

2

📲 Turn on two-step sign-in

It adds a second check after your password. An authenticator app is generally safer than text messages. Start with email, banking and social accounts.

3

🎣 Learn to spot phishing

Scam messages create urgency, ask for codes or contain links that do not match the real site. When unsure, open the official app or type the address yourself.

4

🔄 Keep everything updated

Updates fix security holes attackers already know about. Turn on automatic updates for your phone, computer and apps.

5

💾 Back up what matters

Keep important files in more than one place, such as a cloud service and an external drive, so a lost device is an inconvenience, not a disaster.

🛡️ Quick takeaways

  • One password per account
  • Two-step sign-in on key accounts
  • Pause before you click
  • No genuine company asks for your codes

✅ Do

  • Use a password manager
  • Enable two-step sign-in
  • Check website addresses
  • Install updates promptly

❌ Avoid

  • Sharing one-time codes
  • Clicking links in urgent messages
  • Reusing passwords
  • Ignoring update reminders

Scam checker: what it looks like and what to do 🔍

📧 Phishing email

Looks like: an urgent warning with a link to "verify" your account.

Go to the official site directly and check there.

🎁 Fake prize message

Looks like: you "won" something you never entered for.

Delete it. Real prizes do not ask for fees.

📞 Impersonation call

Looks like: a caller claims to be your bank and asks for a code.

Hang up and call the number on your card or official site.

🛒 Fake shop

Looks like: prices far below normal and no clear contact details.

Check reviews and use trusted payment methods.
