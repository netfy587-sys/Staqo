Stay safe online in five steps

Most online problems come from a few common mistakes. Most fixes take one afternoon.

1

🔑 Use strong, unique passwords

Reusing one password means a single leak can expose everything. Use a long passphrase for each account and let a reputable password manager remember them.

2

📲 Turn on two-step sign-in

It adds a second check after your password. An authenticator app is generally safer than text messages. Start with email, banking and social accounts.

3

🎣 Learn to spot phishing

Scam messages create urgency, ask for codes or use links that do not match the real site. When unsure, open the official app or type the address yourself.

4

🔄 Keep everything updated

Updates fix security holes attackers already know about. Turn on automatic updates for your phone, computer and apps.

5

💾 Back up what matters

Keep important files in more than one place, such as a cloud service and an external drive, so a lost device is an inconvenience, not a disaster.

🛡️ Quick takeaways

  • One password per account
  • Two-step sign-in on key accounts
  • No genuine company asks for your codes

Scam checker: spot it, then stop it 🔍

📧 Phishing email

🚩 Red flagUrgent warning with a link to "verify" your account.
✅ Do thisGo to the official site directly and check there.

🎁 Fake prize

🚩 Red flagYou "won" something you never entered for.
✅ Do thisDelete it. Real prizes do not ask for fees.

📞 Impersonation call

🚩 Red flagCaller claims to be your bank and asks for a code.
✅ Do thisHang up and call the number on your card.

🛒 Fake shop

🚩 Red flagPrices far below normal and no clear contact details.
✅ Do thisCheck reviews and use trusted payment methods.
